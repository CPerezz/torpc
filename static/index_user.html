🧅 TorPC

Connect your wallet to Ethereum, privately, through Tor.

1 Install the local TorPC client

The client runs on your machine, listens on http://127.0.0.1:8545, and tunnels your wallet's JSON-RPC requests through Tor to this hidden service. It also strips wallet-fingerprinting headers (User-Agent, Origin, Sec-CH-UA-*) before they leave your machine.

Download binary Build from source

After installing, run:

torpc-proxy start --config torpc-proxy.toml

What does this protect against?

TorPC routes your wallet's JSON-RPC traffic through Tor, hiding your IP from the operator of this hidden service. The local proxy strips wallet-fingerprinting headers (User-Agent, Origin, Sec-CH-UA-*, Cookie, Authorization) before forwarding, so the operator can't link your transactions to a specific wallet by HTTP fingerprint.

The operator still sees your transaction contents and can choose what to do with them. Trust them as you would any RPC. TorPC does not protect against malicious wallets, traffic-correlation attacks against Tor itself, or smart-contract MEV.
